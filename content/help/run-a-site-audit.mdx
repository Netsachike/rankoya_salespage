The **Audit** page checks your site's SEO health and AI readiness.

## Run the audit

Your first audit takes about a minute. The page shows how many pages were checked and when.

## Read the scores

You get an overall score and a score per category:

- **Technical**
- **Core Web Vitals**
- **Answer-first content**
- **Citation signals**
- **Freshness**
- **Structured data**

Each issue is listed with its severity and a plain-language explanation of what it means and how to fix it.

## Approve fixes

When Rankoya can fix an issue itself, the issue is marked **Fix ready**. Use **Fix issues on your site** to review them together.

These fixes change your live site, so Rankoya only starts after you approve, in the app or on Telegram. Once you approve, Rankoya starts right away and tells you when each change is live. Then verify the live page.

Issues Rankoya can't fix itself stay on your list, marked as yours, with guidance on what to do.
