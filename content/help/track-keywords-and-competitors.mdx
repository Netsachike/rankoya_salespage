## Rankings

The **Rankings** page shows daily Google positions for your tracked keywords.

- **Add keywords**: add the searches your customers make. Rankoya checks them in Google every day.
- **Device**: positions are recorded for desktop and mobile.
- **Average position**: lower is better. It covers the last 30 days.
- **Position bands**: how many keywords sit in each band, so you can see how many are near page one.
- **Trend**: each keyword shows its 30-day trend and change.

If you connect [Google Search Console](/integrations/google-search-console), rankings also show real clicks and impressions. If you disconnect it, rankings go back to Rankoya's own checks.

## Competitors

On the **Competitors** page, add up to five competitors. Rankoya adds them to every scan and comparison.

- **Share of voice**: your visibility next to each competitor, per engine.
- **Questions they win, you don't**: competitors mentioned and you absent, in two or more engines. A gap can go straight to Content as a draft.
- **Keywords they rank for, you don't**: in Google's top 10 for a competitor and outside the top 50 for you.
- **Where competitors get cited and linked**: Rankoya can't earn these links for you, but it drafts the pitch for each one.
