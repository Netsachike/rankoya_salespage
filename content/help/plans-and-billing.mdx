## Plans

- **Free**: AI visibility and Google rankings tracking.
- **Growth**: adds competitor share of voice, the site audit with approved fixes, keyword research, the content plan, articles drafted by Rankoya and approvals on Telegram.
- **Agency**: everything in Growth, plus team collaboration, several brands per workspace, client-ready reports, the agency resource kit and autopilot modes.
- **Enterprise**: everything in Agency, plus the reseller console, reseller accounts, unlimited workspaces and team members, and dedicated partner support.

**White Label** is a one-time licence to sell Rankoya under your own brand: Starter (100 client licences), Pro (300) or Unlimited.

Compare everything on the [pricing page](/pricing).

## Payment

Payments are handled securely by Paddle.

## Changing or cancelling

Go to **Plans** in the app to upgrade, switch or cancel. If you cancel, your plan won't renew and you keep access until the end of the current period.
