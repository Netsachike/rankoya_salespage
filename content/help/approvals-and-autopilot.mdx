Rankoya proposes; you decide. Every change it wants to make arrives as an **approval card**.

## Approval cards

Each card shows what Rankoya wants to change and why. You can:

- **Approve** it, and Rankoya starts right away
- **Edit** it first, so it's worded your way
- **Reject** it

Cards appear in the app and, if you connect [Telegram](/integrations/telegram), in your Telegram chat with Rankoya. Both show the same conversation.

## Autopilot modes

Choose how much Rankoya does on its own. You can change the mode at any time.

- **Ask me first** (default): Rankoya drafts articles and fixes. Nothing goes live until you approve it.
- **Auto-publish content**: articles publish on schedule. Changes to your site still need your approval.
- **Full autopilot**: Rankoya publishes and fixes on its own and sends you a summary after each change.

## The action plan

The **Actions** page lists what to do next, ranked by impact. Rankoya handles its part; the rest is marked as yours. When you approve several of Rankoya's changes at once, it confirms first, because they change your live site.

## Asking the agent

On the **Agent** page, ask about rankings, competitors or content. It is the same conversation as on Telegram.
