Open **Integrations** in Rankoya to connect the tools it publishes to, measures with and talks through. Each integration has its own page with the access it needs and step-by-step instructions.

## Publishing

Rankoya publishes the articles you approve, and on WordPress it also applies approved on-page fixes such as titles and meta descriptions.

- [WordPress](/integrations/wordpress): an application password
- [Shopify](/integrations/shopify): an Admin API access token, for your store's blog
- [Webflow](/integrations/webflow): a site API token and one CMS collection
- [Wix](/integrations/wix): a Wix API key and site ID, for your Wix blog
- [Ghost](/integrations/ghost): an Admin API key

Without a connected platform, you can copy finished articles over by hand.

## Google data

Both use a read-only Google sign-in in a new window. Rankoya never posts on your behalf, and you can revoke access from your Google account at any time.

- [Google Search Console](/integrations/google-search-console): real queries, clicks and impressions. Use **Sync now** to pull fresh data.
- [Google Analytics](/integrations/google-analytics): sessions and conversions from search and AI answers, by page.

After signing in, pick the property that belongs to the brand you are working on.

## Approvals on Telegram

[Telegram](/integrations/telegram) lets you approve work and talk to Rankoya away from your desk. Scan the code or open the chat, press **Start**, then confirm in Rankoya.

## Who can change integrations

Workspace viewers can't change integrations. Ask a workspace admin.
