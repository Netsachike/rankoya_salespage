The **AI visibility** page shows how often AI engines mention and cite you, and where you rank in their answers.

## Add your questions

Add the questions your customers ask. Rankoya asks each one to five AI engines in every scan and rechecks them daily. For help choosing, read [How to choose the questions to track](/blog/choosing-questions-to-track-in-ai-answers).

## Choose the engines

Rankoya checks ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews. Under **Integrations → AI engines** you can pause an engine. A paused engine drops out of your visibility score until you track it again.

## Read the results

For each question and engine you see:

- **Mentioned or not**, and how many of the five engines mention you
- **Position**: where your brand appears in the answer
- **Citations**: the pages the engine cites
- **Sentiment**: whether answers that mention you are positive, neutral or negative

## Sources AI engines cite

This list shows the pages engines cite for your questions, and whether each one mentions you, is missing you, or is your own site. Being mentioned on these pages raises your odds of being cited. Select **Turn gaps into tasks** to add outreach tasks to your action plan.

## Close a gap

Open a question you miss to see Rankoya's plan for it: what closes the gap, and who does each part.

Your first scan finishes in about three minutes after you add your site.
